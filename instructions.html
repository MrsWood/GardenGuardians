Garden Defense: How To Play

Protect the vegetable garden from invading bugs. If vegetables are eaten, your Vegetables Remaining drops. Reach 0 and the run ends.

Goal

  • Use the Level selector to choose a map/level before starting.
  • Survive all waves in the selected level to complete it.
  • Your score is based on highest wave survived (cleared).
  • Highscore tie-breaker: higher unspent money wins.

Controls

  • Left Click empty grass: place selected tower.
  • Left Click tower: select tower and show its range.
  • Right Click tower: upgrade tower.
  • Upgrade Panel: use Upgrade, Sell, and per-tower Target controls.
  • Level: choose the level/map before starting a run.
  • Difficulty: choose Easy / Normal / Hard in the top controls.
  • Start Wave: begin a new wave immediately. Starting early during countdown gives a +25% early-start cash bonus (based on wave-clear bonus).
  • Target: Garden / Closest: switches spray targeting mode.
Waves auto-start 10 seconds after a clear if you do not start one manually. Starting early awards a +25% early-start cash bonus (based on wave-clear bonus).

Bonuses

  • Early Start Bonus: start the next wave before countdown ends to gain +25% to that wave's clear bonus.
  • Flawless Bonus: clear a wave with zero vegetables lost to earn extra flawless cash (shown in Wave Summary and the Flawless HUD indicators).

Defenses

Sprayer Tower ($25)
  • Fires a fan-shaped aerosol burst.
  • Rotates to track target direction.
  • Targeting modes:
    - Closest enemy in range
    - Closest to Garden in range
Glue Pot Tower ($35)
  • Throws sticky trap patches onto paths.
  • Bugs crossing glue are slowed.
  • Upgrades improve trap life, radius, and slow power.
  • Some enemies have glue resistance, so slows are partially reduced.

Gate Crasher (Boss)
Appears every 5th wave with very high HP, armor, and glue resistance.

Boss Waves

  • Boss wave frequency depends on difficulty (Easy: 6, Normal: 5, Hard: 4).
  • The Gate Crasher spawns with support enemies on one path.
  • HUD marks boss waves and wave-clear text confirms boss defeat.
  • The final wave of each level has larger attackers with 100% more strength.

Difficulty Effects

  • Easy: more starting money, softer waves, weaker bosses.
  • Normal: default balance.
  • Hard: less starting money, tougher waves, stronger/more frequent bosses.
  • Changing difficulty resets the current run.

Garden Mechanics

  • Map style: prairie garden with swaying prairie grasses and black-eyed Susans along the paths.
  • When a bug reaches a gate, it moves to a vegetable and starts eating.
  • Each vegetable takes 8 bites to fully disappear.
  • After finishing one vegetable, that attacker leaves the screen.
  • Vegetables Remaining starts at the real count on screen (currently 21).

Special Event

  • Bunny Attack: a large bunny randomly pops up in grass.
  • If it pops on a tower position, that tower implodes.

Highscores

  • At game over, if your run qualifies for top 10, the highscore panel appears.
  • Enter your name and submit.
  • Stored locally in your browser using localStorage.
Attribute values are expected to evolve. This guide is the baseline for current gameplay behavior.
